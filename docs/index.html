💡 Student Guide & FAQ

❓ Is this really 100% free?

Yes! FreeFakeStudio is 100% open-source, and Google Colab provides free access to NVIDIA T4 GPUs for everyone with a Google account.

❓ Can I close the Colab tab?

Keep it open in the background! You do not need to look at it, but if you close the tab completely, Google will shut down your free GPU runtime.

❓ Which model should I pick first?

Start with Z-Image Turbo. It downloads in under 2 minutes and generates beautiful images in just 8 steps!

❓ How do I get back here once Colab finishes?

When the Colab script finishes loading, a giant glowing button will appear right inside Colab: "Open FreeFakeStudio Web App". Click it, and you will be returned here with everything connected!
